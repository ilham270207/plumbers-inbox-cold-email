A cold email of exactly 120 words (subject line included) from a bookkeeping service to a busy plumber: it knows his week, offers one small concrete thing, reads in a minute on a phone between jobs, and ends with one easy ask.

## Why this email

The email opens inside the plumber's actual week — 6 a.m. callouts, invoices scribbled in the van, receipts in a glovebox — so it reads like it was written by someone who knows the trade, not a template. The offer stays small and concrete (sort receipts, chase invoices, tidy tax, one clean number a month), the first month is free, and there is no big contract to fear. It ends with a single easy ask: one 10-minute call, Tuesday at 7 a.m., before the first job.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
